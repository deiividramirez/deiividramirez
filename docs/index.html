PEER-REVIEWED JOURNAL ARTICLE · JULY 2024 · MDPI ROBOTICS 13(8), 115 DOI: 10.3390/robotics13080115

Vision-Based Formation Control of Quadrotors Using a Bearing-Only Approach

David L. Ramírez-Parada, Héctor M. Becerra, Carlos A. Toro-Arcila, and Gustavo Arechavaleta

Formulated a leader–follower multi-quadrotor control law relying exclusively on onboard monocular visual measurements without global position estimation. Leaders navigate via decoupled image-based visual servoing using invariant moments, while followers preserve a translation- and scale-invariant geometric formation under camera field-of-view rigidity constraints — allowing the team to scale dynamically and cross narrow window apertures.
